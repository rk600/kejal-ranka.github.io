Sustainability

AI-Enhanced Emissions Analytics

VIZCON 2025 — 3rd Place (EMEA) • CO₂ modeling • EV transition simulation

Challenge

Last-mile logistics accounts for a significant portion of urban CO₂ emissions. Fleet operators lack data-driven tools to quantify the environmental impact of their route decisions and understand the ROI of transitioning to electric vehicles — especially when traffic and weather conditions introduce significant variance.

What I Built

An analytics platform that models CO₂ emissions from delivery fleet operations, incorporating real-world multipliers (traffic, weather, vehicle type) and simulating EV transition scenarios with AI-generated voice narration to guide dashboard interpretation.

Core Components

  • Emission Modeling: Calculated per-delivery CO₂ based on distance, vehicle type, load factor, traffic conditions, and weather impact
  • Variance Quantification: Identified 26% emission variance attributable to traffic and weather — a previously unmeasured factor
  • EV Transition Simulator: What-if analysis projecting fleet-wide impact of partial and full EV adoption
  • AI Voice Narration: Generated audio walkthrough of dashboard insights for stakeholders who prefer narrative over visual interpretation
  • Decision Storytelling: Transformed raw logistics data into sustainability strategy insights with clear recommendations

Key Insights

Finding: Traffic and weather conditions introduce a 26% variance in emissions that fleet operators typically don't account for. Route optimization that considers these factors could reduce emissions by 12-15% before any vehicle transition — a "free" improvement.
  • Full EV transition projects 58-60% emission reduction with 40% operational cost savings (fuel + maintenance)
  • Partial transition (30% EV fleet) still achieves 22% emission reduction — viable for budget-constrained operators
  • Weather-adjusted routing alone can reduce emissions by 12-15% with zero capital expenditure

Why This Matters

This project demonstrates the intersection of analytics and sustainability — showing how data-driven decision tools can make environmental action financially compelling, not just morally imperative. The AI voice narration component makes complex data accessible to non-technical fleet managers who drive actual adoption decisions.
