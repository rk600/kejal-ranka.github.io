Entity Ranking & Recommendation Engine

Composite scoring system • 10K+ entities • $15M decisions influenced

Problem

An operational team needed to identify and prioritize the highest-impact entities (sellers) for targeted intervention across multiple quality dimensions. Previously, this required manually cross-referencing 5+ data sources, and different teams used different criteria — leading to inconsistent targeting and missed opportunities.

Solution

I built a composite ranking engine that scores 10,000+ entities across multiple weighted dimensions, producing a unified priority list that directly drives campaign selection.

Scoring Formula

Score = (Defect_Volume × Unit_Cost_Weight) + Post_Process_Impact + Pre_Process_Impact Dimensions: • Quality defects × value per unit (cost signal) • Post-processing reimbursements (financial signal) • Pre-processing violations (compliance signal) Configurable: • Include/exclude individual defect types • Trailing window (3-4 months on closed transactions) • Dimension-level scoring weights

Key Design Decisions

Multi-Dimensional vs Single Score

Initially stakeholders wanted a single defect-based ranking. I advocated for multi-dimensional scoring because entities with moderate defects but high financial impact were being missed. The composite approach captures both quality AND financial signals — surfacing the true "top opportunity" entities.

Configurable Weights

Rather than hardcoding weights, I built the system with filter-based configuration. Teams can adjust which dimensions contribute to the score, enabling the same engine to serve different operational programs (quality coaching, financial recovery, compliance enforcement) without code changes.

Automated Weekly Refresh

Rankings update automatically on a weekly cadence using trailing 3-4 month windows on closed transactions. This ensures the list reflects current state, not stale data — critical for operational teams making real-time decisions.

Design principle: A ranking system is only as good as its adoption. I designed the output format to match exactly how the operations team consumes data — pre-filtered by region, sortable by score, with one-click export for campaign systems.

Impact

  • Enabled launch of "Top Opportunity" targeting stream — focusing on $15M in cost-out opportunity from highest-impact entities
  • Replaced manual cross-referencing of 5 disparate sources with single unified view
  • Directly drives weekly campaign selection for 3 operational programs
  • Adopted by cross-functional teams within first week of launch

Lessons Learned

  • Simplicity wins adoption: The first version had 7 dimensions — too complex for operators to understand. Trimming to 3 clear dimensions with intuitive weights drove immediate adoption.
  • Explainability matters: Adding dimension-level breakdowns (not just total score) let operators understand WHY an entity ranked high — building trust in the system.
  • Refresh cadence is a product decision: Daily was too noisy (rankings flipped constantly), monthly was too stale. Weekly on trailing windows was the sweet spot.
