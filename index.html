04 — About

A bit about me

I'm a Data & AI Engineer at Amazon, where I own analytics for EU marketplace programs — the kind where "getting the number wrong" means millions in misallocated resources.

What drives me: taking messy, unstructured problems and building systems that solve them repeatedly, not just once. My best work — a self-improving NLP pipeline — was invited to the EU Leadership Summit in Luxembourg because it showed how AI can be applied to real operational problems, not just demos.

Before Amazon, I spent 4 years at TCS building analytics for Citibank's global operations — Tableau dashboards, IBM Cognos pipelines, and leading a small team of analysts.

I believe the best data engineers are the ones who ask "is this metric even measuring the right thing?" before optimizing it.
